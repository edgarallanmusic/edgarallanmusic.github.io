I built my nest on the highest tree —
the one they call the Future;
the eagles bring what's good for me,
and nothing unclean gets through here.
The wind cuts like an honest word,
the cold burns bright and keeps me young;
I stacked the silence stone by stone
until the view was mine alone.

The hunger taught me how to taste;
now I devour life and leave no trace.

This air that burns
is the cleanest thing I own —
solitude's a weapon
you can only hold alone.

I walk old roads with lighter feet;
the best thoughts find me out of doors.
The ice is close, the sun is sweet,
and down below the valley snores.
It isn't hate, it's just the air —
I simply can't breathe what they share;
a sense of cleanliness, that's all:
I smell a lie from way up there.

The hunger taught me how to taste;
now I devour life and leave no trace.

This air that burns
keeps the borrowed voices low —
solitude's a weapon
only clean hands get to hold.

It isn't steel, it doesn't strike,
it's a door I choose to close;
I guard myself with distance,
I heal myself by breathing slow.
Naked and free, I'll meet the day
and laugh the way the morning does.

Not hiding. Breathing.

This air that burns
is the cleanest thing I own —
one must be made for it...
I was made for it alone.
