Some day my name will smell of thunder,
a crisis written in a quiet hand;
I don't want followers, I don't take vows —
I'd only have to wash my hands.
I'm too full of mischief to believe
even in the man inside these clothes;
I smelled the lie before you told it —
all my genius lives in my nose.

I contradict like no one ever has,
and still I am the biggest yes.

I am not a man, I am dynamite,
and the fuse is made of laughter;
don't you ever call me holy —
I'm the clown who's coming after.

Tap an idol with a hammer, hear the hollow ring;
a thousand years of lying wore the crown of truth;
I'm the bringer of a joy that never had a name,
and the mountains and the valleys swap their views.
I don't want a temple, I don't want a shrine,
I'm writing this before they try;
the first decent man was a scandal in his time —
I'd rather be ridiculous than lie.

Don't make me holy. Please.

I am not a man, I am dynamite,
and the fuse is made of laughter;
I said no to every idol
just to say yes to what comes after.

I am not a man... I am dynamite. They wanted a saint. I'd rather be a clown. Maybe I am a clown. But a clown who brings good news... a harbinger of joy like none before. The mountains and the valleys swap their places... and the idols fall without a sound. Have you understood me? Dionysus... versus the Crucified.
