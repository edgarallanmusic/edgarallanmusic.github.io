I watched the ideals freeze like statues old,
compassion turned to chains, forgiveness cold;
I cut the cord that tied me to their lies —
a cleanliness of soul, my law, my prize.
Resentment found no room in me to stay,
revenge got bored and quietly walked away;
round the corner a saint froze in his prayer —
I didn't push him, I just let in the air.

No more halos on the wall,
just a torch that doesn't flicker;
I carry the clean knife of truth,
and every lie gets thinner.

The weak called it cruel, the strong called it free;
I called it truth, and it called back to me.
I don't refute an idol — I just pull my gloves on,
and lay another error gently on the frost;
the genius freezes first, the hero in his ice —
it's a war that leaves no smoke and asks no sacrifice.
I owe the past no guilt, I leave its debt unpaid;
I keep a steady light, and truth's cold blade.

No more idols made of clay —
let them learn what legs are for;
I carry the clean knife of truth,
I don't swing it anymore.

...it freezes.

Even the faith turned into frost,
and nobody raised a hand;
the cold did all the work for me,
and I could finally stand.

Yes — to the cold and to the light,
to everything the blade has shown;
I carry the clean knife of truth:
it cut me first, down to the bone.
