For the picture

Licensing

All Edgar Allan Music releases are available to license for film, television, trailers, games, podcasts and brand work. Every record begins with a classic text, from Orwell and Hesse to Nietzsche and Dante, and is built to sit under picture.

What you receive

The full instrumental of every track. The vocal version where one exists. Marked loop points for game use. Stems on request. 15, 30 and 60 second edits on request.

One stop

Edgar Allan Music controls the recordings and the compositions, so a single agreement clears both sides.

Terms

Non-exclusive by default, exclusivity by arrangement. Worldwide. Term and fee quoted per project. Payment via PayPal.

Not permitted

  • Political or electoral campaigns.
  • Adult or sexual content.
  • Content promoting hate, discrimination or real-world violence. Fictional violence in games and film is fine.
  • Tobacco, vaping, weapons or gambling advertising.
  • Misinformation or deceptive deepfakes.
  • Resale or redistribution of the recordings as samples or a sound library.
  • Use of the music to train AI or machine-learning models.

Good fit

Trailers, character reveals, end credits, menus, and any scene that needs dread with a brain behind it.
